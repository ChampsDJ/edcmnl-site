Happy Spring! We kick off the changing of the season with a bit of dubstep feels, some electro vibes, and old school breakbeats to set the mood. Next we have a set featuring all of the artists playing at Ultra. Sprinkle in a bit of trance, techno, and house to round it all out and what do you get? Music Night LIVE Vol 9 of course!

Unfortunately for the VODs, our usual screen capturing method failed us due to electrical difficulties caused by big wind storms. For this month, we just have a collection of down-sized VODs from the raw set videos themselves rather than the stream capture.

Here's a link to the [EDC Discord Music Night LIVE Twitch channel](https://twitch.tv/edcdiscordmusicnight)! Give us a follow to be notified when we go live!
