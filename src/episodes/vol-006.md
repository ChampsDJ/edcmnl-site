It's hard to believe that we've been producing this event for six months on the EDC Discord. Thank you for tuning in, for downloading the VODs, for watching our restreams, or just your support in general.

To our host Ronin, we appreciate all you do in putting this together. Papabear, without your A/V prowess behind the scenes, we have no stream.

We had a pretty wide variety of genres this month. Champs covered deep house, Kazami had the tech house sound on lock, AVILA and RHF came at us with some hypnotizing trance, Delanada made us make dirty bass faces all set long, and HXADCBL brought us home with some rawstyle to put a nice hardstyle bow on the night.

See you all in January!
