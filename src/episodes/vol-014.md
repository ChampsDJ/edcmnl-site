September. The beginning of fall, pumpkin spice, way too early Christmas sales, and more vibes straight from the EDC Discord.

For vibes this month, we're featuring: mainstream, dubstep/trance/dnb, tech house, latin house, breaks, and techno with a sprinkle of dnb. As usual, we're ticking all the boxes on the open genre format of our show!

Speaking of which, next month, we have a format change! Our production team (Ronin and Papabear) have been hard at work and this month have debuted a number of changes to the overall presentation of the show. This is continuing next month where we will have space to feature 8 sets, at 45 minutes each.

We hope we see you live with us on Twitch and on the EDC Discord! Here's a link to the [EDC Discord Music Night LIVE Twitch channel](https://twitch.tv/edcdiscordmusicnight)! Give us a follow to be notified when we go live!
