Happy 20 days until EDC Las Vegas 2023! There's no better way to celebrate than the last EDC Discord Music Night LIVE until after EDC!

Kazami and RAAIN started out strong with some tech house beats. Steve hit us with some hard techno from outer space. Champs hit the way back machine to classic trance from the year 2000 and before. C0mp1l3r threw down some hardstyle, techno, and happy hardcore. PapaDCBL wrapped things up with a dirty versus set which set PapaBear vs HXADCBL in a psytrance, techno, and hardstyle showdown.

Here's a link to the [EDC Discord Music Night LIVE Twitch channel](https://twitch.tv/edcdiscordmusicnight)! Give us a follow to be notified when we go live!
